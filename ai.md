# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science focused on building systems that can perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence. These tasks include understanding language, recognizing images, making decisions, and learning from experience.

Modern AI relies on **machine learning**, where models discover patterns in large datasets rather than following explicitly programmed rules. **Deep learning**, built on layered neural networks, powers breakthroughs in translation, medical imaging, and conversational assistants.

AI promises enormous benefits, from accelerating scientific discovery to automating tedious work. However, it also raises important questions about fairness, privacy, safety, and accountability. Responsible development helps ensure AI benefits everyone.
